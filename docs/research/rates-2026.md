Researched 2026-10-20. **How this was checked:** the sandbox could not open any Swedish site directly. skatteverket.se, scb.se, ekonomifakta.se and riksdagen.se all failed with DNS or proxy 403 errors. Every figure below therefore comes from web-search result extracts, which quote the source pages. Where a primary page (Skatteverket, regeringen, Riksdagen, Riksrevisionen, Medlingsinstitutet) was quoted in the extract, the figure is marked **[primary]**. Figures that came only through aggregator sites quoting SCB are marked **[secondary]**. My own derivations and judgement calls are marked **[estimate]**.
